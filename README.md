### / About

I am a Computer Engineering student at the Xavier Institute of Engineering, driven by a philosophy of learning through building. I don't just collect technologies; I integrate them into cohesive systems. My focus lies at the bleeding edge of engineering—where software meets hardware, data fuels intelligence, and digital twins mirror reality.

---

### / Currently Building

- **GEOCAD Digital Twin** &nbsp;·&nbsp; A scalable 3D digital-twin platform representing real-world environments digitally, starting with a prototype of Lodha Park (React, Three.js, PostgreSQL).
- **IoT-Based Blind Curve Collision Warning System** &nbsp;·&nbsp; A wireless road-safety system leveraging ESP32 and ESP-NOW to detect and warn against approaching vehicles on blind curves.
- **Applied Machine Learning Models** &nbsp;·&nbsp; Exploring feature selection, DBSCAN, and predictive modeling using Python, Pandas, and Scikit-learn on real-world datasets.
